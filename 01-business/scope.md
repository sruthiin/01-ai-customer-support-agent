# Scope and Assumptions

## In scope
- Order-tracking guidance
- Cancellation information
- Refund information
- Payment issue guidance
- Damaged or wrong product reporting
- Basic account-support guidance
- Ambiguous request clarification
- Human escalation

## Out of scope
- Real database integration
- Actual order lookup
- Actual refund processing
- Real payment transactions
- Changing sensitive account information
- Production deployment

## Assumptions
- All scenarios and data are synthetic.
- Public LLM interfaces may be used.
- The model has no real ShopEase database access.
- Human support is available for escalation.
- Model name/version/date should be recorded when visible or available.
