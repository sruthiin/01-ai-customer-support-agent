# Business Problem

## Scenario
ShopEase is a fictional e-commerce company receiving a high volume of repetitive customer-support requests.

## Problems
- High volume of repetitive queries
- Delayed responses during peak periods
- Inconsistent first-level guidance
- High manual workload for routine questions
- Risk of poor customer experience when responses are inaccurate or unclear

## Business objectives
- Improve first-response speed
- Provide more consistent first-level guidance
- Reduce routine workload
- Improve clarification and escalation behavior
- Prevent fabricated or unsupported information

## BA interpretation
The core business need is not simply “build a chatbot.” It is to define and validate safe, useful first-level support behavior with explicit boundaries and human escalation.
