|---|---|

| Business Analyst | Clear requirements and behavior definition |
| AI / Engineering Team | Implementable prompt and workflow requirements |
| QA / AI Quality Analyst | Reliable testing and defect identification |
| Risk / Compliance | Safe behavior and boundary controls |
